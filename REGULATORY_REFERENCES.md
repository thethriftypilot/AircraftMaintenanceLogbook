# Regulatory reference and implementation matrix

Reviewed against official eCFR and FAA pages on **October 30, 2026**. eCFR pages displayed currency through October 28–29, 2026, with Title 14 last amended October 15, 2026. The whole-Part-43 URL initially returned an access challenge; individual official sections/appendices were readable. No access-control bypass was used. Current regulations prevail over older guidance. This review supports implementation boundaries, not legal certification.

## RC recheck, 2026-10-30 (America/New_York)
Official sections/appendices and FAA advisory PDFs above were opened again for this stabilization pass. This is an engineering source review, not independent A&P/IA or legal acceptance. Relevant paragraph mapping: §43.9(a)(1)–(4); §43.11(a)(1)–(7), (b); Appendix A(c), especially (23) for oil strainers/filter elements (task eligibility still depends on all applicable conditions); Appendix B(a)–(d); §91.207(c)(1)–(2), (d) and applicable exceptions; §91.409(a)–(f); §91.411(a)–(b); §91.413(a)–(c); §91.417(a)–(b); §65.95(a)–(c). The review did not establish applicability to any real aircraft.

Inspection language is generated only on an explicit user request, then shown for acceptance and marked for substantive review. Nonapproval includes a dated list and owner/operator delivery language. Progressive inspection distinguishes routine and detailed scope. The app's text checks identify selected contradictions; they are not a natural-language compliance engine. A user can still enter a legally insufficient or untrue statement. Independent qualified review remains mandatory before public operational release.
