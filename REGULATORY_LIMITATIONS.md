# Regulatory and signature limitations

This is an evaluation build of a recordkeeping tool, not an FAA-approved system or a determination of airworthiness. An independent qualified aviation review is outstanding. Reference checks were performed October 30, 2026; this is not a commitment to continuous regulatory updates.

The software cannot determine actual work quality, approval eligibility, aircraft operating limitations, certificate authenticity/currency, rating scope, maintenance manual compliance, AD completeness/applicability, or whether a statement is factually true. It validates fields and selected workflow constraints, not every legal condition. In particular, inspection statement text and program applicability require human review.

Drafts, finalization and PDF generation never execute an approval. Generated PDFs explicitly remain unsigned. Users print and physically sign under applicable authority. Recording an externally signed scan records evidence and the recorder's name; it does not authenticate that signature or create a new electronic signature. Hashes and local append-only events are limited tamper evidence. A person with filesystem/database administration access can replace the database, executable or hashes. No independent timestamping, PKI, identity verification or AC 120-78B compliance claim is made.

Major repairs/alterations may require separate FAA forms and disposition. The application stores copies and does not generate official forms or perform submissions. Discrepancy-list delivery is user affirmed; the app cannot verify actual delivery or required placarding. Deferred does not authorize an MEL/91.213 deferral. Closed does not authorize flight.

Scheduling reflects recorded thresholds only. Applicable exceptions, inspections after equipment work, manufacturer limitations, ELT battery-use/life rules, 100-hour excess-time calculations and specialized programs must be reviewed independently. Do not substitute tach/Hobbs for time in service without a valid basis. Unknown values remain unknown. No automatic AD search, airworthiness judgment or signature compliance is offered.

Retain original legally required records and independently verified backups. Local records are not automatically purged. Complete handover archives contain all aircraft; selected-aircraft archive packaging remains deferred.
